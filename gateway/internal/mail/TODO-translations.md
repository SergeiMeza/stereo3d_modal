# Mail copy still to translate

English ships first (owner 2026-11-08). Every key below exists only in
`copy/en.json`; a mail for an account in another language renders in
English until that language's `copy/<lang>.json` ships (`mail.Resolve`).

Languages waiting: zh-Hans, ja, fr, de, zh-Hant, ko, es, ru, tr, ar (Arabic is right-to-left: set `_meta.dir` to `rtl`).

Translate only when a release is being prepared. A first draft of all ten
languages was made on 2026-11-07 and parked outside the repo; reuse it, then
check the keys marked *changed* against the English below.

- *changed* `dl1.balanceLost`: now ends "when your account is deleted".
- *changed* `dl1.subject`: the date is now day and month only.
- *new* `reset.reason`.
- *removed* `dl1.refundAsk`, `dl1.refundLink` (refund policy 2026-11-08): drop them from the drafts.

`TestTranslationTodoListsMissingKeys` fails when a key is missing from a
shipped language and isn't listed here.
